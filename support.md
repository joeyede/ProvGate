## Common Issues

**App shows "Not connected"**
- Check your internet connection
- Verify your MQTT broker is running
- Try logging out and back in

**Gate doesn't respond to commands**
- Ensure the gate-remote device is powered on and connected
- Check that your HiveMQ broker credentials are correct

**Siri / Shortcuts not working**
- Open ProvGate and make sure you are logged in
- Re-add the shortcut in the Shortcuts app
