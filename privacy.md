# ProvGate Privacy Policy

ProvGate does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The app stores your MQTT broker credentials (username and password) locally on your device using the platform's secure encrypted storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). This information never leaves your device except to authenticate directly with the HiveMQ broker.

No analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection of any kind is used.
